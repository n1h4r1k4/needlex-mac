# Code of Conduct

NeedleX should be a friendly place for everyone who uses it or works on it, whatever their
background, identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- Credit others' ideas and work when you build on them in a discussion.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a shared space.

## Where it applies

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies
anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their
GitHub profile, or by opening a private security advisory on this repository.
Reports are handled confidentially.

## What happens next

The maintainer may respond with a private warning, by removing comments or contributions, or, for
serious or repeated problems, with a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
