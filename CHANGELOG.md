## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-15

The first macOS release: one download for Apple silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 13+).

- **Island** around the notch for Spotify and Apple Music, with a cover, title, a real-audio
  visualizer, a "now playing" peek, scrubbing and controls. Choose the notch or pill shape,
  optional liquid glass, and drag the island to move it.
- **Island pages:** player with synced lyrics, weather (Open-Meteo, rough location) and calendar
  (Calendar app). Switch between them with a two-finger swipe.
- **Lyric effects:** Big Words, Lens, Stars, Terminal, Aura and Scrolling lines, in the cover's
  colours. Each can use a Simple, Glass, Liquid or Foggy background, with the album cover beside
  the lyrics if you like. Songs without synced lyrics still get an effect.
- **Desktop wallpaper:** lyrics while music plays, or a turntable, record sleeve or cover with
  aura that you can drag and click.
- **Lock-screen player:** record → big cover → lyrics. It fades away while you type your password.
- **Full screen** cover and lyrics view, opened from the island or after a few idle minutes.
- **Beat glow** around the screen edge in the cover's colours, and optional glitter.
- **Light on the battery:** animations pause when nothing moves and run at a capped frame rate.
