# Changelog

Notable changes to NeedleX for macOS. Dates are in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Unreleased

### Added
- Lyric effects: Big Words, Lens, Stars, Terminal, Aura and Scrolling lines, with Simple, Glass,
  Liquid or Foggy backgrounds and an optional album cover beside the lyrics.
- Desktop wallpaper: lyrics while music plays, or a turntable, record sleeve or cover with aura.
- Lock-screen player: record → big cover → lyrics. It fades away while you type your password.
- Island pages for weather (Open-Meteo) and calendar (Calendar app), switched with a two-finger
  swipe.
- Beat glow in the cover's colours.
- Songs without synced lyrics still get an effect (plain lyrics spread across the song, or the
  title and artist).
- A local signing identity so macOS keeps NeedleX's permissions across rebuilds.

### Changed
- The weather page asks for your location from NeedleX itself. macOS never answered the helper's
  request, so the page waited forever.

### Removed
- The live lyric line under the island.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-15

- First macOS version: a music island around the notch for Spotify and Apple Music, with synced
  lyrics, a full-screen view, liquid glass, an audio visualizer and an edge light.
